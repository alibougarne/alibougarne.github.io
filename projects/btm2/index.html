BTM2

Senior full stack Java/Angular developer

Client: SPF Finances

Jan 2020 – Jun 2022

A payment management program carried out for the Belgian Federal Public Service Finance (FPS), made of three parts:

  • BTM2-CODA: a microservice that manages the receipt of the FPS Finance account statements from Bpost
  • BTM2-PAY: a microservice that retrieves the payments to be made by the FPS Finances
  • BTM2-INSTAPY: a microservice that receives instant payments from citizens and saves them in a database

Stack: Spring Boot/Spring Cloud, Hibernate, JPA, DB2, Maven, Flyway migrations, JUnit, Mockito, Angular, Bootstrap, Angular Material, Karma, Jasmine, OpenAPI, RabbitMQ, Microservices, Git/GitLab, Scrum, Jira, Confluence.
