Global Axis

Web developer

Client: Dypix

Jan 2014 – Dec 2016

A website built at Dypix for Global Axis, the distributor of Bridgestone tyres in Algeria. It presents the whole range to drivers, fleets and businesses, and it is still online.

  • Catalogue: tyres by category (passenger cars, SUV and 4x4, run-flat, truck and bus, civil engineering, industrial) and by range
  • Advice: how to maintain and check tyres
  • Company pages: Bridgestone, Global Axis, a contact page and careers
  • Available in French and Arabic

Technologies
