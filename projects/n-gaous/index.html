N'gaous

Web developer

Client: Dypix

Jan 2014 – Dec 2016

A website built at Dypix for N'gaous, an Algerian company that processes fruit into juices, fruit drinks and preserves, with two production units (N'gaous and Menaa). The site is no longer online: the link on this page opens its copy in the Internet Archive.

  • Presentation of the company and its production units
  • The range of products, and recipes to cook with them
  • Contact and recruitment pages
  • Available in French and Arabic (right-to-left), with a link to the company's Facebook page
