Nreservi

Web developer

Client: Dypix

Jan 2014 – Dec 2016

A website built at Dypix for Nreservi.com, an Algerian online travel agency. Travellers search, compare and book their stay or trip online and pay in Algerian dinars. The site is still online.

  • Hotels: search and booking in Algeria and abroad, with discounted stays
  • Flights: booking of airline tickets from Algeria
  • Organised tours: packages to popular destinations, and travel ideas by theme (beach, romantic, family, visa-free)
  • Professional space: a separate area for travel professionals
  • French-language site with a promotional news ticker, a search form on the home page and a guide on how to book

Technologies

  • PHP
  • Bootstrap
  • MySQL
  • Photoshop
  • Joomla
  • jQuery
