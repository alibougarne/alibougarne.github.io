SmartHire-Ai

Author

Client: SmartHire AI

Jan 2025 – Sep 2026

Hiring, matched by AI. SmartHire AI pairs candidates with the roles they fit using AI-generated match scores, and gives recruiters a focused dashboard to review, score and move applicants forward. Built on my own and deployed on OpenShift.

  • AI-powered matching: every application is scored against the job description, so candidates and recruiters both know where they stand
  • Apply in seconds: one candidate profile, reused across every job
  • Recruiter dashboard: track applicants by status, leave private notes and move candidates through the pipeline
  • Try it: sign in as the demo recruiter (recruiter@techcorp.com / recruiter123) or register as a new candidate
  • Continuous delivery: GitHub webhooks trigger Tekton pipelines that build and ship every change

Stack: Spring Boot, Angular, PostgreSQL, OpenShift, GitHub webhooks, Tekton.
